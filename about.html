ABOUT DE LOUNGE INN

Who We Are

Located within the serene Lagoon View Estate in Ikorodu Lagos State DE Lounge Inn is designed for guests who appreciate comfort elegance relaxation and premium hospitality. The hotel blends luxury accommodation with entertainment dining and nightlife to create an experience that feels exclusive yet welcoming. Every corner of the property is thoughtfully designed to provide peace comfort and beautiful aesthetics.

Guests enjoy a calm environment away from the noise of the city while still remaining connected to Lagos nightlife social activities and business opportunities.
